# Royal Degeneracy: the cast

The eight people who lived at 282, and two regulars who didn't but were always there. Side characters (friends who
play as guests) come later. How the cast fits into the game is in [DESIGN.md](DESIGN.md); this file is who they are.

The house is called **282** in the game. The number on its own is fine; the street name never goes in.

This file lives in the private repo. The shipped game (`cast.ts`, which anyone on gambol.lol can download) uses the
name tags below and leaves out everything marked **Stays here**. Show each person their card before it ships.

How the values come from your answers:

- **Drinking**: heavy is a Booze rating of 6.5 or more, light is under. Heavy drinkers leave a pile of empties; light
  drinkers leave one warm, half-finished can.
- **Footprint**: big is 185 cm and up, medium 175, small 170 and under.
- **Room**: for Nash and Shota it's their own place's mess level: something from there, left at the scene.

All ten cards are different, so a full set of real clues always pins down one person. Will Palmer and JJ match on
the first six; their hair (medium brown against short black) is what tells them apart.

### Tells

Some characters give themselves away and can't switch it off:

- **Will Palmer's cologne**: every room he's been in smells of it for 20 s after he leaves. Stand still for 2 s to
  sniff, and you'll know he was just there.
- **Shota overheats**: once a night, at a random moment, he gets too hot and takes his shirt off, and it drops
  wherever he's standing. A shirt on the floor tells you Shota came through, and it might be the one lying next
  to a body.
- **Wags's cigarettes**: he's the only one who smokes them, so a cigarette butt at a crime scene points straight at
  him. Nobody will believe it, and the Degenerates will plant them constantly.

## The housemates

### Dev (Devlin)

*Captain of the concrete toboggan. The tidy, chill one.*

- **Studies** Mechanical Engineering.
- **Looks**: 185 cm, longer brown hair.
- **Who he is**: chill, not much of a bro, keeps his room very tidy, has a minor temper, and knows his way around
  crypto. Venezuelan-Canadian.
- **Room**: very tidy.
- **Ability: Toboggan Run.** Hops on the team's concrete toboggan and blasts down a staircase or the length of a
  hall at 3× walking speed. It's loud: everyone on that floor hears it coming. 45 s cooldown.
- **Quirk: Engineer.** Fixes sabotages twice as fast.
- **Catchphrase**: none; he nods.
- **Lines** (drafts): "Captain's orders." · "My room's clean, just saying." · Evicted: "You'll regret this on race
  day."
- **Stays here**: girlfriend Kaitlin.

### Mikol (Michael)

*Has never once been angry. Quietly a crypto genius.*

- **Studies** Computer Science.
- **Looks**: 185 cm, medium-length brown hair.
- **Who he is**: about as chill as a person gets, no temper at all, not a bro. Knows crypto better than anyone in
  the house. South African-Canadian.
- **Room**: moderately messy, and a little stinky because Shota is in there a lot.
- **Ability: Router Logs.** He set up the house WiFi. At the router he sees which room every phone was in, scrubbed
  back over the last 90 s. He also gets into locked phones in 2 s. 30 s cooldown.
- **Quirk: Unbothered.** The fire alarm doesn't shake his screen, and the bathroom gross-out doesn't time him out.
- **Catchphrase**: "What bro." Also what he says when the meeting turns on him.
- **Lines** (drafts): "It's all good." · "I'm not selling." · Evicted: "What bro."
- **Stays here**: girlfriend Jess; how much he made.

### Jung (Andrew)

*The maths brain, with a short fuse for nonsense.*

- **Studies** Applied Mathematics Engineering.
- **Looks**: 175 cm, medium-length black hair.
- **Who he is**: top of the class, fairly chill, a middling bro. Gets properly annoyed when people do illogical
  things. Korean descent.
- **Room**: usually messy, but not too bad.
- **Ability: Proof.** Pick a player and a crime: the game checks them against every *real* clue posted from that
  crime and answers "consistent" or "contradiction". Plants can't fool it. Twice a night.
- **Quirk: Illogical!** His phone buzzes the moment two posted clues from the same crime can't both be true, which
  means one of them was planted.
- **Catchphrase**: "Wagwan."
- **Lines** (drafts): "That's not logical." · "Explain to me how that makes sense." · Evicted: "This is
  statistically ridiculous."
- **Stays here**: girlfriend Nicole.

### Xang (Angus), sometimes Bongus

*The kindest, humblest man alive, until he becomes Bongus.*

- **Studies** Mining Engineering. Loves rocks, and is generally very smart.
- **Looks**: 175 cm, short brown hair.
- **Who he is**: the ultimate humble, kind person. Never angry no matter what, zero bro. After the biggest bong rips
  he becomes Bongus.
- **Room**: complete chaos. Clothes everywhere, a dismal desk setup.
- **Ability: Bongus Mode.** One enormous rip and he's Bongus for 15 s: his name tag reads BONGUS, he walks slower,
  the world goes hazy, and hidden clues within 6 m glow through walls. Twice a night.
- **Quirk: Rock Hound.** Searches twice as fast. He knows where to dig.
- **Catchphrase**: "Yoooooo." As Bongus, a lot more o's.
- **Lines** (drafts): "No worries at all." · "Has anyone seen my rock?" · Evicted: "All good guys, love you."

### Selby (Jack)

*Runs Bike Stu, a studio full of people nobody else has met.*

- **Studies** Teaching.
- **Looks**: 175 cm, longer red-orange hair.
- **Who he is**: chill, zero bro, makes rap songs with his friends in Bike Stu, the makeshift studio in his room,
  usually with people nobody else in the house knows. Respects everyone. Only gets angry when something truly dire
  has happened. Has been found passed out on the toilet more than once.
- **Room**: chaos, and stinky. A raccoon lives on the covered balcony outside it.
- **Ability: Bike Stu Session.** Two randoms from his studio wander the house for 45 s. Everyone sees them; nobody
  knows who they are. If either sees a crime, Selby gets a text with one trait of whoever did it ("yo some really
  tall guy just bonked someone in the kitchen"). Twice a night.
- **Quirk: Dire.** When a crisis sabotage hits, he can fix it alone; it normally takes two.
- **Easter egg**: taken out in the bathroom, he slumps onto the toilet. Nobody is surprised.
- **Catchphrase**: none; he nods.
- **Lines** (drafts): "Come through to Bike Stu." · "Okay. This is actually dire." · Evicted: "Bike Stu will
  remember this."
- **Stays here**: girlfriend Meg; his politics.

### Wags (Lucas)

*Genuinely the nicest person ever. Stressed about everything.*

- **Studies** Commerce. Very focused, and always worried about his grades.
- **Looks**: 190 cm, the tallest in the house. Short brown hair.
- **Who he is**: constantly stressing about random things, a bit of a mama's boy, a little scared of the world.
  Would help anyone at any time and cares deeply about every one of his friends. Never angry, just disappointed.
  Smokes the odd cigarette indoors, completely out of character.
- **Room**: clean, with the record player where everyone gathers to listen after a night of drinking. The end of
  every night happens here: the reveal plays out in Wags's room with a record spinning.
- **Ability: Look Out.** Guards a friend for 30 s: they can't be taken out, and he's told if someone tries. 45 s
  cooldown.
- **Quirk: Worrier.** A crime anywhere on his floor makes his phone buzz, with an arrow to it for 3 s.
- **Catchphrase**: "How's it goin."
- **Lines** (drafts): "I'm not mad, I'm just disappointed." · "Guys, is this safe?" · "Records in my room after?" ·
  Evicted: "...I'm just disappointed."

### Will Palmer

*Campus famous. Somehow gets great grades. Somehow never at the house clean.*

- **Studies** Mechanical Engineering.
- **Looks**: 165 cm, the shortest in the house. Medium brown hair. Cares a lot about how he looks: sharp clothes, a
  lot of cologne, and a drink always in his hand.
- **Who he is**: the ultimate socialite: everyone on campus knows him and loves him. Maximum bro, not very chill,
  gets angry at people. Has alcohol sponsorships, so the liquor is free. A nice guy who can be a little devious,
  and has a habit of missing the communal clean-up.
- **Room**: impeccably clean.
- **Ability: Campus Legend.** Once a night at a meeting, his vote counts double, and everyone sees it.
- **Quirk: Supervisor.** His own chores take 50% longer, but anyone doing a chore within 3 m of him works 50%
  faster. He's great at getting other people to do the work.
- **Tell**: the cologne (see Tells).
- **Catchphrase**: "How's it going, buddy."
- **Lines** (drafts): "Drinks are on my sponsor." · "I was literally about to help." · Evicted: "You'll all be at
  my party next week anyway."
- **Stays here**: the homework story (Supervisor tells that joke without saying it); his love life.

### JJ (Jacob)

*The responsible one. Has everything figured out.*

- **Studies** Chemical Engineering.
- **Looks**: 170 cm, short black hair. Cares about his clothes, just less than Will.
- **Who he is**: by far the most responsible person in the house. Ultra nice, polished, super kind. Easy-going
  until he finds a dirty kitchen or someone not pulling their weight. Gets angry, in a controlled way, at things
  worth getting angry about. Never smokes.
- **Room**: impeccable.
- **Ability: Inspection.** Stands in a room for 3 s: anything in it that was moved or planted sparkles. 40 s
  cooldown.
- **Quirk: Spare Keys.** Of course he has the spare keys: he opens locked doors and locked clues in 2 s.
- **Catchphrase**: "Hey, how are you?"
- **Lines** (drafts): "Whose dishes are these?" · "We have a chore chart for a reason." · Evicted: "Fine. But
  someone clean the kitchen."
- **Stays here**: girlfriend Lauren.

## The regulars

They don't live at 282 but they're always there, so they're main characters too. They have no bedroom in the house:
each wakes up where he always is.

### Nash

*Zero bro, ten out of ten green. Staying in.*

- **Studies** Commerce. A commerce student with a bro level of 0, somehow.
- **Looks**: 170 cm, very short brown hair.
- **Who he is**: super relaxed and chill. Barely drinks, though he will with the boys. Mostly stays in, smokes and
  plays games. Gets angry at dirt, filth and people doing stupid things.
- **His room** (not at 282): clean, with a lot of weed paraphernalia.
- **Wakes up** on the 282 sofa with a controller in his hand.
- **Ability: Wallhack.** He's played enough games: for 3 s, everyone within 8 m shows as an outline through walls
  and floors. Twice a night.
- **Quirk: Couch Lock.** Sitting on the living-room sofa, he hears every footstep in the house at full volume,
  walls or not.
- **Catchphrase**: "Yo."
- **Lines** (drafts): "One more game." · "Who left this here? That's disgusting." · Evicted: "Cool. I'll be on the
  couch."

### Shota

*Shirt off. Always down. Never, ever angry.*

- **Studies** Sciences. Quite smart, but bad at doing his homework.
- **Looks**: 185 cm, very long black hair, the bigger build of a rugby player. Usually shirtless by the end of the
  night (see Tells).
- **Who he is**: the chillest person anyone knows, and always down for any fun activity. Plays rugby. Smokes an
  immense amount of weed; doesn't drink much. Never gets angry: the most he'll do is "Broooooo what broooooo."
- **His room** (not at 282): a mess, and barren, and slightly smelly.
- **Wakes up** in Mikol's room, which explains the smell in there.
- **Ability: Ruck.** Bursts through a locked door, and lifts the two-person chores (the sofa, the mattress) on his
  own. 30 s cooldown.
- **Quirk: Always Down.** Anyone can ping him from their phone, and he gets an arrow to them. If Shota's the
  Degenerate, they've just called their killer over.
- **Tell**: he overheats and drops his shirt (see Tells).
- **Catchphrase**: "Broooooo no broooooo." Accused, shoved, or taken out: "Broooooo what broooooo."
- **Lines** (drafts): "I'm down." · "Who's down?" · Evicted: "Broooooo what broooooo."

## Cases from the house's own stories

New mysteries these suggest, on top of the Drumset Massacre, the Sink of Shame and the Unflushed. I'll write them out
in full; swap in real stories whenever you want.

- **The Scratched Record**: Wags's favourite record, ruined, in his own room.
- **The Sponsored Stash**: Will Palmer's free liquor, all drunk. The weapon is an empty bottle.
- **Who Let the Raccoon In**: the raccoon is loose inside all night, trashing the place and running off with clues.
- **The Indoor Cigarette**: the house reeks of smoke. Everyone suspects Wags. The culprit gets extra cigarette
  plants.
- **The Toboggan Job**: the concrete toboggan, vandalised days before race day.
- **The Missing Geode**: Xang's best rock is gone.
- **The Great Clean-Up**: it's communal clean day and someone keeps undoing it (finished chores reset).
- **The Rage Quit**: someone threw Nash's controller through the telly.
